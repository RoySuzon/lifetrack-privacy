LifeTrack Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-17
Publisher: Goutom Roy
Privacy and support contact: chotodeal.inbox@gmail.com

LifeTrack helps you record personal goals, habits, focus time and reflections. The app works offline and does not require an account.

Data collection and sharing

LifeTrack does not send your personal data to the publisher or to an advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service. The app has no backend, advertising SDK or Internet permission. We do not sell your data.

Information on your device

LifeTrack stores your optional display name, goals and schedules, progress, focus sessions, notes, journal and mood entries, weekly reviews, checklists, milestones, rest days, themes and preferences locally. It uses this information to show your dashboard and history and schedule the reminders you choose. These records are not made public or sent to other users.

The app's private storage is protected by Android's application sandbox. LifeTrack does not add its own password or database encryption. Home-screen widgets and notifications can display goal names, progress and focus details; your launcher and lock-screen notification settings affect their visibility.

Backup and restore

You can explicitly export a backup and choose its destination using Android's file picker. A backup is a readable JSON file and is not encrypted by LifeTrack. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file according to its own terms and privacy policy. The publisher does not receive the file. Restore reads only the backup you select.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled in LifeTrack's configuration. Keep exported files somewhere you trust and delete copies you no longer need.

Notifications and permissions

Optional reminders and focus/break alerts are scheduled on your device. LifeTrack requests notification permission when needed. Optional Android Alarms & reminders access allows more precise delivery; alerts may arrive later without it. Boot access restores scheduled alerts after a restart. You can change reminders in LifeTrack or disable notification/alarm access in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them, use Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall LifeTrack. The publisher holds no copy and cannot recover deleted records. Exported backups are independent files; deleting app data or uninstalling does not delete those files. Remove them separately from the destination you selected.

Support messages

If you contact the support address yourself, the publisher receives the email address and contents you send and uses them to respond to your request. Do not include private journal entries or backup files unless you intend to share them. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policy.

Children

LifeTrack does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

Policy changes will be posted at the same public address with a revised effective date. Send privacy questions to chotodeal.inbox@gmail.com.
