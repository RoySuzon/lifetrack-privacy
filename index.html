Daybini Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-18
Publisher: RoyCraft
Contact: chotodeal.inbox@gmail.com

Daybini is a personal goal and habit tracker that works offline. You do not need an account.

Data collection and sharing

Daybini processes your records on your device and does not automatically send them to the publisher or an advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service. The Android release has no backend or Internet permission. We do not sell your data. Backups you choose to export and support emails you choose to send are described below.

Data on your device

Your optional name, goals, schedules, progress, notes, focus sessions, mood and journal entries, weekly reviews, checklists, milestones, rest days, themes and preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage. This includes focus presets, reminder, quiet-hour and widget preferences. Android's app sandbox protects this storage; Daybini does not add a password lock or database encryption. Bini's moods, tips and celebrations are selected on your device from activity progress, focus state and local time; they do not use a remote AI service or send your entries for analysis.

Backups you export

Android automatic cloud backup and device transfer of app data are disabled. You can export a backup through Settings to a location you choose in the system file picker, including a cloud provider. Backups are readable, unencrypted copies of your records and preferences. Anyone with access to a file can read it. Daybini does not receive the file; your chosen storage provider handles it under its own policy. Restore reads the backup you select and replaces the app's current local data after confirmation.

Optional notifications

Goal reminders, weekly summaries, review reminders, focus and break alerts are scheduled locally. Daybini asks for notification permission before showing notifications. Precise timing uses optional Android Alarms & reminders access, with inexact delivery if access is unavailable. Scheduled alerts can be restored after a device restart. Notifications, including supported Android live updates, may display goal names, progress and focus details. You control lock-screen visibility and can revoke notification or alarm access in Android settings. Home-screen widgets also display selected goals, completion and focus details; use the widget's hide-details control to conceal activity names. Your launcher controls who can see the widget on your home screen.

Retention and deletion

Use Settings → Delete all data to erase the app's local data, or clear app storage or uninstall the app. Exported backup files are separate copies: delete them from the locations where you saved them. We hold no copy and cannot recover deleted data.

Support messages

If you email the support address, the publisher receives the email address and content you send and uses them to respond. Do not send private journal entries or backup files unless you intend to share them. Your email provider handles delivery under its own policy.

Public policy website

The policy is readable inside the app without network access. If you open its public website in a browser, the hosting provider processes the network information needed to serve that page, under its own privacy practices. Visiting the page does not upload your Daybini records.

Children

Daybini does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Policy changes

If data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date. Check the public policy listed on the Play Store for the latest version.
